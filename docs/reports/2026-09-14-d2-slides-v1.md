# d2-slides v1: four writers green, and what a green deck still cannot prove (2026-09-14)

## Question

Can a fresh agent, given one brief and `d2-slides`' `SKILL.md` alone — no
builder source, no worked example, no other attempt — produce a deck that
passes the four page gates and still says what the brief asked for?

Procedure: `agent-validation-loop`. Fixture `fixtures/d2-slides-scenario/`.
Each brief carries its own **required content** list (strings that must appear
in the built `copy.txt`) beside the gate runs, so a deck that is green and lost
half the brief is visible — the lesson `anim-ir` learned in its round 13.

Four writers, four decks that met their done condition; three on the first
write, and the small model needed one fix round. Every required string landed
in every deck, and every brief's figures were present.

That is the honest headline and it is also the problem with it: **the round
found nothing by failing.** Everything below came from what the writers said
after they were green, and from dogfooding the fixture's own deck.

## What didn't — the five findings

### 1. A figure that compiles can draw the wrong thing, and no page gate can tell

Two writers, on different briefs, named this independently. `a`:

> "the skill never says how to verify a figure's *content* (not just that it
> compiled) against a brief that specifies what the figure must show — the
> builder's '3 figure(s) laid out by tala' line and passing gates don't
> guarantee the api/data figure actually draws the edge from the root with full
> paths as the brief requires, versus some other wrong-but-compiling shape."

`d`, from the other side, on two figures that must agree:

> "A reader who only skimmed slide 2 … could plausibly leave slide 1's figure
> unchanged and every gate here would still pass — `check integrity`,
> `check copy`, and `check a11y contrast` are all blind to whether two figures
> agree with each other. … If the brief hadn't been explicit, the discrepancy
> would have shipped clean."

Root cause: the check they are asking for already exists — `d2-facts.mjs
--from-svg <slide>.svg --expect <sheet>` — and the skill mentioned it as an
aside after the done condition ("when a figure has facts it must not get
wrong"), not as a step. An aside after the finish line is not in the loop.

**Fixed in this commit:** it is step 7 of the loop and a line in the done
condition, with the sheet's shape shown. And the answer to `d`'s version is
written down: one sheet held against *both* slides' SVGs is what pins two
figures to each other. Verified on `d`'s own attempt — the shared sheet
`{"boxes": ["checkout","ledger"], "deps": ["checkout->ledger"], "forbidden":
["checkout->orders"]}` passes both its figures, and fails the un-updated
original with `✗ box not drawn: ledger` and exit 1.

### 2. The copy gate had no "must not appear" half

`d` had to leave the tool to finish its own brief:

> "`check copy --manifest` only detects missing required lines, never
> forbidden/stale ones — I verified the deleted closing line was actually gone
> with a manual `grep -F`, since the brief's 'must no longer contain'
> requirement has no gated equivalent."

Its own proposal was the fix: "add a `--forbid <file>` mode".

**Fixed in this commit** — `vlmkit check copy --forbid <file>`, new rule
`copy-forbidden`. Deliberately *not* symmetric with `--manifest`: the manifest
is matched against the visible text, the forbid list against the raw text and
every revealed disclosure state, because a stale claim behind a `<details>` or
left at `font-size:0` is still shipped. The report names where it was found, so
hiding the line does not satisfy it. Six analyzer tests; end-to-end it exits 1
naming the stale line on the un-edited deck and 0 on `d`'s.

### 3. Nothing said what the copy manifest covers

`b` spent its one fix round on this and reached the wrong cause:

> "The skill says markdown-formatted text in D2 diagrams can fail extraction …
> but doesn't say the real answer: **move required content outside D2 diagrams
> into plain Markdown prose**. I had to discover this by testing."

`b`'s explanation — that `**` inside a d2 label breaks extraction — is not what
happens; the manifest is generated from the Markdown prose, so a figure's
labels were never candidates. The misdiagnosis is the evidence: the skill was
silent, so a writer inferred a mechanism. `d` wanted the same fact from the
other end, and recorded it as a file it wanted to open:

> "`build-deck.mjs`: wanted to confirm whether copy-manifest text is derived
> purely from rendered DOM text … the skill doc doesn't say whether speaker
> notes count toward the copy manifest at all."

**Fixed in this commit:** a section saying the manifest is the Markdown prose
only — a figure's labels, the speaker notes and the footer chrome are not in
it — and therefore that a string the deck must be *seen* to say belongs in
prose, while a figure's claims go to step 7.

### 4. "~17 elements per slide" read as a threshold

`c`:

> "I got 43 across 6 slides (~7.2/slide), below both the brief's own
> rule-of-thumb and SKILL.md's stated '~17/slide'. I had to independently
> re-run the gate against `index.html` (got exactly 4) to confirm 43 really
> meant 'all slides on print.html'."

The number came from one deck (this skill's own example, 133/8) and does not
generalize: it tracks how many text nodes a deck has. `c`'s workaround is the
better instruction — the comparison between the two views, not the absolute
count.

**Fixed in this commit:** the done condition names the comparison and gives the
observed range as a range, not a target.

### 5. `near-misalignment` fires inside the figure's SVG, and cannot be exempted

Found by dogfooding the fixture's own re-edit deck before any writer ran, which
is why no writer quote appears here: `check integrity` on `print.html` returned
`NO DEFECTS, 2 WARN`, both

> `[near-misalignment] g.KGdhdGV3YXkgLSZndDsgY2hlY2tvdXQpWzBd @1280: … is off
> its siblings' shared top edge by 5.5px inside svg.d2-4071436177.d2-svg`

— two of TALA's own edge-label groups, 5.5px apart. Three things about it:

- It is a **warn**, so the command exits 0. The skill's done condition said
  `CLEAN`, which a deck with a labelled edge may never be: that was an
  unreachable line in the skill, and the briefs inherited it.
- It **cannot be exempted stably.** `--allow` matches the finding's own
  selector by substring, and that selector is the base64 class d2 derives from
  the edge's text (`(gateway -> checkout)[0]`), so any exemption breaks when
  the label is edited. An ancestor selector does not work: measured,
  `--allow "near-misalignment@.d2-svg;…"` exempts 0 findings and is reported as
  matching nothing.
- It is **layout-dependent, not label-dependent.** `a`'s deck has labelled
  edges and is CLEAN; three probe decks with one, two identical and two
  different labels are all CLEAN; `d`'s edit of the warning deck made the warn
  disappear. So "avoid edge labels" is not the workaround and would be bad
  advice.

**Fixed in this commit:** the done condition says no *failures*, and a failure
mode entry says what the finding is, that it exits 0, and that
`--rule near-misalignment=off` is the only lever and costs the rule for the
slide chrome too.

**Deferred:** making `--allow` match an ancestor subtree (`@.d2-svg` for "this
is a third-party render, do not inspect its internals") is the principled fix
and belongs to the exemption matcher in `integrity-exemption.ts`, not to this
skill. It needs findings to carry a path or the matcher to consult the DOM, and
no writer has yet been blocked by it — `d` hit the deck and came out CLEAN.

## Also observed

- `b`'s round-1 compile errors were `timeline:` (a construct it invented) and
  `_.api` at the root — the second is documented in `d2-diagram`'s failure
  modes, which `d2-slides` routes figure problems to. A routed skill is not a
  read skill; the reserved-word list that did land is in `d2-slides` itself.
- `d` read the deck cold and found the format legible where it matters — "slide
  boundaries (`---` on its own line)… bullet-vs-quote (the `>` marker is
  unambiguous)" — and named one real ambiguity the format does not carry:
  "**Slide insertion point is unstated by the format.** `---` on its own line
  separates slides but carries no semantic ordering hint." Recorded, not fixed:
  a deck is read start to finish and the ordering *is* the document order.
- The scorer's own briefs carried a bug the writers caught: the "at least 100
  inspected elements" floor was written from the example deck and is wrong for
  a six-slide deck. Relaxed to a per-slide rule of thumb before the round, then
  removed from the skill entirely per finding 4.

confirmed as a real class rather than an argument. If neither does, the next
investment is not another writer round — it is the deferred exemption matcher,
or a vision reader on the slide images, which is the measurement this loop
does not yet have for a deck.
