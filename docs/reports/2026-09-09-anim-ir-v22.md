# anim-ir v22: the diff figure — two module maps as one still, with the change marked (2026-09-09)

## Question

A module map says what is. A change to a system is two maps, and the reader
of a pull request wants one picture: what came, what went, what moved. Item
seven on the user's list was the diff figure. This round adds it — drawn from
two scenes, so the picture of the change is derived, not drawn by hand — and
asks the usual two questions: does a writer produce the right one from the
guide alone, and does a reader get the change back from the pixels.

## What was added

- **`vlmkit-anim diff before.json after.json [--out change.svg|.png] [--scene change.json] [--expect diff.json]`.**
  The picture is the **after** map. What it added — modules, dependencies, a
  container — is in the accent colour; what it lost is drawn in where it was,
  dashed and grey (a removed module inside the container it left, when that
  container survives); a module that changed container, or label, is accent
  too; a legend in the readout panel counts the change. The change is also
  data — `diffFacts`: `added` / `removed` (modules, deps `"a->b"`, groups),
  `moved` (`{module, from, to}`), `relabelled` — printed in one line:

  ```
  +1 module (search) · +2 deps (api->search, search->db) · +1 group (identity) · −1 module (cache) · −1 dep (api->cache) · 1 moved (auth: core → identity) · 1 relabelled (logging: "logging" → "logs")
  ```

  and `--expect` checks it against a diff sheet (`schema --kind diff`): every
  field optional, a present one exact, a change the sheet does not name an
  error too. A lifted `forbidden` dependency is a removed dependency in the
  facts and is not drawn.
- **`"dashed": true` on a module or diagram node** — a dashed outline for a
  box that is not, or no longer, there. The diff figure uses it for removed
  modules; it came out of the writer round (below).

## What changed from it

- **Removed modules are dashed** (oa): `dashed` on a module or node draws its
  outline `6 4`, in the SVG and in the runtime, and the diff figure sets it.
  The guide and the help text now say what they said and mean it.
- The `modules` and `diagram` schema sheets and guide rows list `dashed`.

## What the round did not settle

- **An edge label beside a removed arrow.** The dashed `api->cache` runs past
  `emits`, the label of `api->logging`, and the reader gave the label to the
  wrong line. Edge labels sit off their own line's longest segment and edges
  are not routed around other edges' labels; a diff figure adds lines (the
  removed ones) that the after map's labels never had to avoid. Routing edges
  round edge labels is a v13-class change to the diagram compiler and waits
  for a second sighting.
- **Accent arrows read weaker than accent boxes.** One reader, one figure:
  the two added arrows were not reported as lit. A thicker stroke for
  accent-toned edges would test it; not done on one sighting.
- **Only `modules`.** A `diagram` pair, a graph pair, a state machine before
  and after a transition was added — each is the same shape of work (facts of
  the difference, the after picture with the difference marked). `modules`
  was the kind whose stills have a corpus and readers; the others wait for a
  brief.
- **`pr` and `diff`.** `vlmkit-anim pr` draws the areas a range of commits
  touched; `diff` draws what two revisions of a map say. A `pr` that drew the
  workspace map's diff between `base` and `head` — `repo` at both, then
  `diff` — is one command away and not asked for yet.
