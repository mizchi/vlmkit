Shipping notes

Parcels packed before noon leave the same day. Larger crates go out on the evening van, and a tracking number is mailed once the carrier scans them at the depot.

Returns are checked within two working days. A refund is issued to the original payment method, and store credit is offered when the item was a gift.

Fragile goods are wrapped twice. If a box arrives damaged, keep the packaging and send a photo of the label so the claim can be filed with the courier.

Bulk orders above forty units ship on pallets. Delivery slots are booked by phone, and the driver calls half an hour before arriving.
