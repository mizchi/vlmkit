Notes from a wet October

The rain came early this year, and with it the fungi: shaggy inkcaps on the verges, a ring of fairy bonnets under the birch by the gate, and one morning a parasol mushroom as wide as a dinner plate.

Most of them are gone within a day. That is the pleasure of it — a walk is never the same walk twice.
