# Product: the Add to cart button

`page.html` is a product card. Clicking "Add to cart" turns the button
green and changes its label to "Added ✓ — View cart", and the button widens
smoothly to fit the new label (300ms, slowing at the end). All through, the
label stays on one line and the button keeps its height.

A reviewer says: "When I click it, the button jumps to two lines and then
snaps back. It looks broken for a moment."

Fix it without changing how the button looks before or after the click.
The viewport is 1280x800.
