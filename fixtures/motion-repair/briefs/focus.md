# Retreat: the hero card

`page.html` is a travel landing page with one large card. When the page
loads, the card comes into focus: it starts blurred and transparent, and over
600ms, slowing at the end, it fades in and sharpens. Once it has arrived, the
card and its text are perfectly sharp.

A reviewer says: "After it loads, the whole card looks a bit soft, like a
slightly out-of-focus photo. The headline isn't crisp."

Fix it without changing how the card comes in, or how it looks otherwise. The
viewport is 1280x800.
