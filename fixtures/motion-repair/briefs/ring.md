# Storage: the usage ring

`page.html` shows how much storage is used, as a ring. Shortly after the page
loads, the blue arc fills smoothly clockwise from empty to 72% over one
second, slowing at the end. The number in the middle says 72% the whole time.

A reviewer says: "The ring doesn't animate. It's empty, and then it's just
full."

Fix it without changing how the ring looks once it has filled. The viewport is
1280x800.
