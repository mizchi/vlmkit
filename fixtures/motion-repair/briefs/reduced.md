# Features: the three tiles

`page.html` is a features page. As it loads, the three tiles rise into place
one after another (400ms each, 80ms apart, fading in). People who have asked
their system for reduced motion get no motion at all: for them the tiles are
simply there from the start, in their final place.

A reviewer, who uses the reduced-motion setting, says: "Below the heading the
page is empty. There's nothing there."

Fix it without changing what anyone else sees. The viewport is 1280x800.
