# Search: the suggestion chips

`page.html` is a search page. Under the search box sit four suggestion chips.
They fade in over 200ms as the page appears — straight away, with no wait
before the fade starts — and then stay.

A reviewer says: "On load there's an awkward moment where the box is there and
the chips aren't. It feels laggy."

Fix it without changing how the page looks once loaded. The viewport is
1280x800.
