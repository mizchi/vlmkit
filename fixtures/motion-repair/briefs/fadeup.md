# Notifications: the list

`page.html` is a notifications page. When it loads, the three notifications
come in one after another, top to bottom, 60ms apart: each one fades in while
rising 12px into place, over 400ms, slowing at the end.

A reviewer says: "They fade in, but they don't rise at all. It used to have a
little lift."

Fix it without changing anything else on the page, including the shared
utility styles' behaviour for the marketing site. The viewport is 1280x800.
