# Long read: the reading progress bar

`page.html` is a long article. A thin red bar across the top of the window
shows how far you have read: it is empty when the page is at the top, fills
from the left as you scroll down, and is full when you reach the end. It
follows the scroll position exactly, both ways.

A reviewer says: "The progress bar is full the moment the page opens and
never changes."

Fix it without changing how the article or the bar look. The viewport is
1280x800.
