# Team: the notification badge

`page.html` has a bell in its header with a red "3" badge. Half a second after
load the badge throbs to catch the eye — it swells and settles back, three
times in a row, each throb taking 600ms — and then it sits still for good.

A reviewer says: "That badge never stops throbbing. It's exhausting."

Fix it without changing how the badge looks once it has settled. The viewport
is 1280x800.
