# Order: the confirmation card

`page.html` shows an order confirmation card. When the page loads, the card
flies in from the left: it starts 200px to the left of its place, tilted 20°
anticlockwise and transparent, and over 500ms, slowing at the end, it slides
straight across to its place while it straightens and fades in. It travels
horizontally the whole way.

A reviewer says: "It comes in at an angle, swooping up from below instead of
sliding across."

Fix it without changing where the card ends up or how it looks at rest. The
viewport is 1280x800.
