# Markets: the live quote

`page.html` shows a live share price. When the page opens, the price fades
up into place once (400ms, slowing at the end). After that the price and the
change below it update every second: the numbers change in place, and
nothing fades or moves again.

A reviewer says: "The price keeps blinking. Every second it fades in again,
like the page is reloading."

Fix it without changing how the card looks, how the price first appears, or
how often it updates. The viewport is 1280x800.
