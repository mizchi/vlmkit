# Welcome: the hero

`page.html` is a landing page with one hero block. When the page loads, the
hero rises 24px into its place and fades in over 500ms, slowing as it arrives.
People who have asked their system for reduced motion see it already in place,
with no motion at all.

A reviewer says: "Nothing moves on this page. I don't have reduced motion
turned on."

Fix it, keeping the reduced-motion behaviour. The viewport is 1280x800.
