# Settings: the sync gear

`page.html` shows a "Syncing settings…" status. The small indigo gear at the
left of the pill turns in place, clockwise, one full turn every 2 seconds,
for as long as the sync runs. It stays where it is drawn and only turns.

A reviewer says: "The gear flies around the box instead of spinning. Half
the time it's not even in the pill."

Fix it without changing how the status looks. The viewport is 1280x800.
