# Storage: the usage bar

`page.html` shows how much storage is used. When the page loads, the blue bar
inside the grey track fills from the left to 72% of the track over 800ms,
slowing as it arrives, and stays there.

A reviewer says: "There's an empty grey track. The blue bar never shows up."

Fix it without changing the bar's colour, height, timing or how full it ends.
The viewport is 1280x800.
