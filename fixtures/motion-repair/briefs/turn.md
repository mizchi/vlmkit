# Flashcards: turning a card over

`page.html` shows one flashcard. A click turns it over to show the answer,
and another click turns it back. The turn takes 600ms, easing in and out, and
is a real turn in depth around the card's vertical axis, as if you watched the
card from about 900px in front of it. The edge swinging toward you grows taller
than the card, and the edge swinging away shrinks. Half way, you look at the
card edge on. At rest each face is flat and upright, and its text reads
normally.

A reviewer says: "The flip looks flat, like the card is just being squashed
sideways and back."

Fix it without changing the card's size, colours, timing or what a click does.
The viewport is 1280x800.
