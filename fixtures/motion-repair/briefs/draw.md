# Payment received: the check mark

in), the green check mark draws itself as one continuous stroke, from the end
of its short arm, through the corner, to the tip of its long arm, over 600ms,
slowing at the end. Once drawn, the whole check mark stays on screen.

A reviewer says: "The tick never finishes. Half of the long arm is missing."

Fix it without changing how the finished check mark looks. The viewport is
1280x800.
