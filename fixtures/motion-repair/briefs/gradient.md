# Plans: the Upgrade button

`page.html` is a pricing card. Its Upgrade button is filled with a gradient,
blue on the left to indigo on the right. When the pointer moves over it, the
gradient shifts smoothly to purple on the left and pink on the right over
300ms, and when the pointer leaves it shifts back the same way.

A reviewer says: "The hover colour just snaps. There's no fade at all."

Fix it without changing how the button looks at rest or under the pointer.
The viewport is 1280x800.
