# Inbox: the "new messages" notice

`page.html` is an inbox. When the page loads, the blue notice at the top
slides in from the left, from 360px left of its place, fading in as it comes,
over 400ms, slowing at the end.

A reviewer says: "It fades in, but it doesn't slide at all. It just appears in
place."

Fix it without changing where the notice ends up or how it looks at rest. The
viewport is 1280x800.
