Lighthouse walk

The harbour opens at six. Boats leave on the morning tide and come back by noon, when the market fills the quay.

Ask at the office for the walking map: the path to the lighthouse is two kilometres along the breakwater.

Tide tables for the week are posted by the gate, and the café keeps a copy behind the counter.

Last ferry to the island leaves at 18:30 in summer and 17:00 from October.
