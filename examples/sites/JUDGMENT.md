### R1 · first look: the gallery as generated (reviewer)

- **N1** decision: The gallery is generated (gallery.mjs) from the Pages manifest and each page's log, so its words and numbers are held by sites.test.mjs; what nobody has checked is how it looks and whether it works. Judged at its published URL (http://127.0.0.1:4190/sites/, the intro server's Pages layout), because its landing and solitaire cards reach ../judgment/ and ../demo-solitaire.png, which only exist in that layout — from file:// two of its eight pictures would be broken for reasons that are not the page's. Gate set, from markup-assist (a static page, no behaviour beyond links): 1. check integrity (1280/768/375) and check a11y contrast — the page's floor. 2. check design, check composition, check color — eight cards must be one component, the headings a real hierarchy, and the purple/blue "by eye / by gate" numbers must not be colour-only meaning. 3. check a11y focus (1280, 375), check a11y touch (AA, AAA), check interactions — about 30 links and nothing else to operate. 4. check breakpoints --sweep and scan scroll (375, 1280) — an auto-fill grid of cards at every width. 5. scan handlers — the protocol's minimum; the page has no script, so it should find nothing. Not gated: dark mode. The page follows prefers-color-scheme alone and no gate can emulate a colour scheme, so its dark theme is judged by eye (shot --dark) and by a computed contrast table of its dark tokens.
