check a11y contrast

Pale text that fails WCAG contrast

Every text-bearing element's colour against the background actually behind it, with the threshold its size earns (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text).

Open the page under test · fixtures/a11y-contrast/low-contrast/page.html

Run it

vlmkit check a11y contrast fixtures/a11y-contrast/low-contrast/page.html

What to look at

The large pale line passes at 3:1 only because it is large, and fails anyway at 2.54:1. Colours the browser serialises as oklch() or lab() are read back from a rasterised pixel, so none are skipped.

The fix

Darken the four inks to at least the ratio each needs.
