A header that grew 48px and pushed everything down

Two versions of one page, compared per viewport: the pixel diff, the computed-style diff that explains it, and a triptych image.

Open the page under test · fixtures/shift-patterns/header-grow.html · baseline.html

Run it

vlmkit diff html fixtures/shift-patterns/baseline.html fixtures/shift-patterns/header-grow.html --output {out}

What to look at

A raw pixel diff says 18.9% changed. The shift detector says one thing happened: everything below the header moved +48px, and after undoing the shift only 5.8% differs.

The fix

Restore the header's padding (the computed-style diff names height, padding-top, padding-bottom).
