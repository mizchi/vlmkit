check color

Links you can only tell apart by colour

The palette by role (surfaces, ink, marks, by painted area) and two WCAG rules. Here the links in the prose have no underline and are 2:1 against the text around them.

Open the page under test · fixtures/color/link-color-only-broken.html

Run it

vlmkit check color fixtures/color/link-color-only-broken.html

What to look at

check a11y contrast passes these links: they are readable against the background. This is the other criterion (WCAG 1.4.1), the link against the sentence it sits in.

The fix

Underline in-prose links, or reach 3:1 against the body ink.
